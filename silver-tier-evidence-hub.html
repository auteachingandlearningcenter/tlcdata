About the Programme

What is Silver Tier Recognition?

A formal institutional acknowledgment of your excellence in teaching innovation, evidence-based practice, and commitment to transforming student learning.

Programme Purpose

  • Recognize faculty who apply evidence-based teaching innovations
  • Celebrate measurable improvements in course design and delivery
  • Support continuous professional development through structured pathways
  • Build a culture of teaching excellence across AU
  • Connect innovative educators in a reflective community

Recognition Pathway

  • Bronze Tier — Initial engagement & digital readiness
  • Silver Tier — Applied teaching practice innovation
  • Gold Tier — Advanced pedagogical leadership
  • Platinum Tier — Teaching excellence exemplar
📌 Key Insight

Silver Tier evidence can be gathered from your existing Moodle courses. You do not need to start from scratch — your innovation is already happening. This process helps you document and celebrate it.

🕐 Time Commitment

Most faculty complete their Silver Tier submission in 3–5 hours of focused work. Evidence collection from existing course activity is straightforward with our templates.

Expert Guidance

Faculty Success Tips

These insights from the TLC team will help you prepare a strong, compelling submission.

1

Keep Reflections Concise & Specific

Focus on one or two specific innovations rather than trying to cover everything. Depth beats breadth. Reviewers want to understand exactly what you changed and why.

2

Focus on Student Impact

Always connect your evidence back to student learning outcomes. The question isn't just "what did you do?" but "what changed for your students as a result?"

3

Use Clear, Annotated Screenshots

Add arrows or highlights to your screenshots to draw attention to the specific features or changes. Before/after comparisons are very powerful evidence.

4

Speak Naturally in Your Video

You don't need a professional recording setup. Use Zoom screen recording or Loom. Talk as if you're explaining your course to a colleague — natural & authentic wins.

5

Highlight Engagement Data

Moodle activity completion rates, quiz attempt statistics, and H5P interaction data are all powerful. Even a simple table comparing semester to semester is compelling.

6

Contact TLC Before Submitting

The TLC team is here to support you. A 15-minute consultation before you finalize your submission can save significant time and ensure your evidence meets expectations.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Submitting blurry or unclear screenshots that can't be reviewed
Writing a reflection that describes what you did but not the impact
Uploading files that exceed size limits (video via link instead)
Forgetting to include student feedback or engagement evidence
Submitting under Route B without checking programme approval first
Missing required form fields (name, college, route selection)
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before submitting your Silver Tier evidence.

Do I need a full teaching portfolio to apply for Silver Tier?
No. Silver Tier does not require a full portfolio. You need focused, specific evidence of one or two teaching innovations — not a comprehensive record of your entire teaching career. The submission is designed to be manageable and targeted.
Can I submit multiple examples or evidence from different courses?
Yes, you may include evidence from more than one course, but it is recommended to focus on your strongest example and present it in depth. Quality and depth of reflection is more important than quantity of examples. Two strong pieces of evidence from one course are better than five superficial examples from five courses.
What happens after I submit? Will I know if my submission is being reviewed?
Upon submission, you will receive an automated confirmation email with your submission reference number. The TLC team will review your evidence within 14 working days. If additional information is needed, the TLC will contact you directly. You will receive a final decision email with feedback regardless of the outcome.
Will TLC provide feedback if my submission is not successful?
Absolutely. If your submission does not meet the criteria, you will receive specific, constructive feedback explaining what additional evidence or improvements are needed. You are welcome to resubmit after addressing the feedback. The TLC is here to support your success, not simply to evaluate it.
How long does the verification process take?
The standard review period is 14 working days from the date of submission. During peak periods (end of semester), this may extend slightly. You will be notified of any delays. If you have not heard back after 14 working days, you are encouraged to contact tlc@ajman.ac.ae with your submission reference number.
Can workshops or individual TLC sessions count toward Silver Tier?
Individual workshops or TLC sessions alone do not qualify for Route B, which requires a structured programme with defined learning outcomes and completion criteria. However, a series of workshops that form a coherent programme may qualify. Contact the TLC to discuss your specific situation. For Route A, workshop attendance can be mentioned in your reflection as development context, but the main evidence must be your applied teaching practice.
Is there a deadline for submitting Silver Tier evidence?
Silver Tier submissions are accepted on a rolling basis throughout the academic year. However, submissions received after a specific semester deadline may be recognized in the following ceremony cycle. Check with TLC for current cycle deadlines to ensure your recognition is included in the next ceremony.
