Teaching Excellence Framework

A Pathway to Professional Mastery

TEF provides a structured, evidence-based progression for faculty to develop, demonstrate, and be recognized for teaching excellence at Ajman University.

🥉

Bronze

Foundational level for faculty beginning their excellence journey. Establishes core competencies in learner-centered pedagogy.

  • Complete TLC Orientation Programme
  • Attend 3+ core PD workshops
  • Peer observation & reflection
  • Teaching portfolio initiation
  • Student feedback analysis
Apply for Bronze →
🥈

Silver

Intermediate recognition for faculty demonstrating sustained improvement, innovation, and leadership in teaching practice.

  • Bronze certification required
  • Lead a Community of Practice session
  • Integrate digital learning tools
  • Course redesign with evidence
  • Mentor a junior colleague
Apply for Silver →
🥇

Gold

The highest recognition for faculty who shape the institution's teaching culture and contribute to global knowledge.

  • Silver certification required
  • Published SoTL research
  • University-wide workshop leadership
  • International collaboration
  • Systemic curriculum impact
Apply for Gold →

Partnerships & Global Engagement

TLC connects Ajman University to world-class pedagogical expertise through strategic UK and international partnerships, elevating our teaching standards to global levels.

UEA
University of East Anglia
🇬🇧 Norwich, United Kingdom
Collaboration on academic professional development frameworks, SoTL methodologies, and student-centered learning approaches. UEA's Centre for Excellence in Learning & Teaching directly informs AU's TEF design.

Queen's University Belfast

UU
Ulster University

Collaboration on employability-focused curriculum design, authentic assessment practices, and graduate outcomes research. Ulster's applied learning philosophy shapes AU's competency-based education approach.
